Pianos are expensive. This is not.

A real piano costs more than a used car. A decent digital keyboard still costs more than a month of rent. Cinephone costs whatever your webcam already cost you, which for most people is nothing extra at all.

It is not going to replace a Steinway any time soon (or possibly ever), but it is just getting started. More notes, better tracking, maybe even full chords are all on the roadmap. Consider this the very early alpha version of your future air orchestra.

The (extremely technical) manual

1

Get comfortable

Hold both hands up, palms toward the camera, fingers spread like you are conducting an orchestra (or surrendering to airport security).

2

Curl to play

Bend any finger toward your palm and it plays a note. Straighten it back out to reset it.

3

Know your range

Left hand covers C4 to F4, right hand covers G4 to C5. Together, that is a full octave of mild musical chaos.
